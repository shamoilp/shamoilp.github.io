    About this library

    Built by Shamoil Plumber as a ready reference for Saudi income tax and Zakat work, written so that a non-specialist can follow it.

    The Arabic texts are the official texts published by ZATCA, and the Arabic prevails. English texts are labelled by source: ZATCA's own translations, the 2004 translation of the Income Tax Law filed with the WTO, or translations and summaries made for this library. Plain-words notes, habits and issue guides are our own.

    Committee principles are quoted from the English volumes published by the General Secretariat of Zakat, Tax and Customs Committees (GSTC) and linked to their pages; links from a principle to an article are made by keyword and are a starting point, not a ruling. The 2023 draft laws are described from firms' public summaries because the drafts themselves could not be downloaded.

    Check the official text before relying on any point. This is not legal or tax advice. No cookies or tracking on this page.
