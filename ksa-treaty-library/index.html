    About this library

    Built by Shamoil Plumber as a ready reference for Saudi treaty work.

    Treaty texts are taken from the PDFs ZATCA publishes. The plain-words guide is written for this library: rates come from the Saudi WHT Checker data, and time limits are read from each treaty's text. The OECD Commentary digest summarises the OECD Model Tax Convention 2025 (Full Version), CC BY 4.0, adapted.

    The BEPS MLI tab works out, treaty by treaty, which MLI rules apply and from when, from the OECD's records of each country's MLI choices, checked against official synthesised texts where a tax authority has published one.

    The Cases tab summarises, in our own words, the decisions in the Saudi tax committees' published volumes that turn on a tax treaty, and rulings in India on the India treaty, each linked to its source. Committee decisions are published without names; the Arabic text prevails over the English translations. Pending cases are not tracked.

    The UAE treaty's English text is an unofficial translation made for this library; the Arabic text is the only authentic version.

    The summaries are paraphrases, not the official texts. Check the original before relying on any point. This is not legal or tax advice.
