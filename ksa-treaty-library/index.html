    About this library

    Built by Shamoil Plumber as a ready reference for Saudi treaty work.

    Treaty texts are taken from the PDFs ZATCA publishes. The OECD Commentary digest summarises the OECD Model Tax Convention 2025 (Full Version), CC BY 4.0, adapted. The KSA view draws on ZATCA's circulars and guidance and Saudi Arabia's MLI positions. Each tab names its sources.

    The summaries are paraphrases, not the official texts. Check the original before relying on any point. This is not legal or tax advice.
